# User-needs interviews (agricultural offices / extension workers)

Purpose: answer the panel's question "who is the application for, and what do they need as output?" with evidence.
Target: 5-10 short interviews (20-30 min) with development agents, woreda/zone office experts, EAA or research staff.

## Before
- Get the permission/ethics step your department requires; use `consent_form_template.md`.
- Explain in two sentences: a photo of a field edge, the system marks suspicious areas for expert follow-up; it is not a diagnosis.

## Questions
1. Role: what do you do about enset disease today? How many farms/kebeles do you cover?
2. How do you currently find EXW? Who reports it, how late is it usually, what does it cost to inspect a farm?
3. Which devices do you and farmers have (phone model, camera quality, internet, power)? Do you have a desktop/laptop in the office?
4. Would you rather: (a) take photos in the field and get an answer on the phone; (b) collect photos and process them in the office? Why?
5. What output is useful: yes/no, a risk level, a marked image, a list of farms to visit? Show the heat-map example.
6. How many false alarms are acceptable before you stop trusting it? Is missing a case worse than a false alarm?
7. Who must confirm a flagged farm, and what happens next (quarantine, tool disinfection, uprooting)?
8. Language and literacy needs for the interface? Any training required?
9. Cost: what would be affordable for an office? For a farmer?
10. Anything we did not ask that matters?

## After
Record each interview in `response_log.csv`; summarise themes in `docs/user_needs/summary.md` (counts of who said what;
no more than a handful of quotes, anonymised).
